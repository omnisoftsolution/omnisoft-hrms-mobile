Fingerprint / Face sign-in.

- Turn it on under Profile > Security & Privacy. When enabling, your password is now checked with the server first — a wrong password is refused.
- After you log out, tap the "Sign in with fingerprint" button on the login screen to sign back in without typing your password (no automatic popup).
- Delete account still requires your password.

Testers: enable it with a WRONG password (should be refused), then the correct one, then log out and sign back in with the button.
